# Changelog

Ditto's versions, newest first. The numbers: **X.Y** adds something (saved programs still open), **X.Y.Z** only
fixes, a new **X** may change how things are saved.

## 1.0 — 2026-10-23

The first release. Ditto is [Golana](https://github.com/Gadlele/Golana) 1.2 turned into a sampler of the
mid-eighties, after the E-mu Emulator II:

- **A library of sixteen sounds**: PIANO, STRINGS, CHOIR, BRASS, FLUTE, CELLO, ORGAN, HARPSI, MARIMBA, BELLS, PIZZ,
  BASS, TIMPANI, ORCH HIT, RECORDER, HARP; 41 zones from CC0 recordings, the choir synthesized, the orchestra hit
  struck together from trumpets, violins and a timpani. Three user slots for your own samples.
- **Two sounds a note**: SOUND A and SOUND B, stacked, crossfaded by the velocity (VEL X) or split at a key; B's
  own SEMI and TUNE; BAL in the patch bay.
- **The sampler's character**: EII (8-bit μ-law, no interpolation), EMU 1 (8-bit linear), CLEAN.
- **START and LOOP**: the notes start further into the sample; a sustained sound looped or played once through.
- **Eight voices** (five when two sounds are stacked or crossfaded), POLY, MONO, LEGATO and UNISON.
- From Golana: five filters (LPF24 as the Emulator II's SSM 24 dB), FILTER EG, AMP EG, two LFOs, four patch cords,
  DRIFT, the arpeggiator, the 16-step sequencer, MOD FX, delay and reverb, four palettes, pages that draw (SOUND A
  and SOUND B draw their sample, its loop and START).
- **32 programs**, 24 factory sounds: STRINGS, CHOIR, ORCH HIT, PIANO, BRASS, FLUTE, CHOIR STRING, HARPSICHORD,
  MARIMBA, TUBULAR BELL, PIZZICATO, UPRIGHT BASS, TIMPANI, PIPE ORGAN, CELLOS, SHAKUHACHI, BOW + PLUCK, BASS / PIANO,
  DEEP CHOIR, HIT ARP, EMU 1 KEYS, AIR CHOIR, TAPE FLUTE, HARP + BELL.
- **A power-on with a record**: it turns, the tone arm sets down, DITTO comes up.
- **USB rescue**: OCT− held at power-on, or after two failed starts.
