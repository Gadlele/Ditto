# Changelog

Ditto's versions, newest first. The numbers: **X.Y** adds something (saved presets still open), **X.Y.Z** only
fixes, a new **X** may change how things are saved.

## 1.0 — 2026-10-23

The first release. Ditto is [Golana](https://github.com/Gadlele/Golana) 1.2 turned into a sampler of the
mid-eighties after the E-mu Emulator II, as dry as it was:

- **A library of sixteen sounds**: PIANO, STRINGS, CHOIR, BRASS, FLUTE, CELLO, ORGAN, HARPSI, MARIMBA, BELLS, PIZZ,
  BASS, TIMPANI, ORCH HIT, RECORDER, HARP; 41 zones from CC0 recordings, the choir synthesized, the orchestra hit
  struck together from trumpets, violins and a timpani. Three user slots for your own samples.
- **The Emulator II's voice**: SOUND A, 8-bit μ-law with each sample held (EII) or CLEAN, START and LOOP; a 24 dB
  low-pass and a VCA, each with its ADSR; one LFO with a delay to the pitch, the filter and the level; velocity to
  the level and the filter. The routings fixed, no patch bay.
- **PRESET DEF**: SOUND B with its SEMI, TUNE and LEVEL, stacked, VEL SW, VEL X, SPLIT or POS X; the arpeggiator.
- **Eight voices** (five when two sounds are stacked or crossfaded), POLY, MONO, LEGATO and UNISON.
- **A grey panel**: a two-line LCD in 5 × 7 dots, the modules and their numbered functions (MASTER, FILTER, LFO/VCA,
  VOICE DEF, PRESET DEF, SEQUENCER, EFFECTS, DISK), four sliders, the modules' LEDs.
- **Delay and reverb**, and a small **16-step sequencer** with ties, accents and slides, its steps on the LCD.
- **32 presets**, 24 factory sounds: STRINGS, CHOIR, ORCH HIT, PIANO, BRASS, FLUTE, CHOIR STRING, HARPSICHORD,
  MARIMBA, TUBULAR BELL, PIZZICATO, UPRIGHT BASS, TIMPANI, PIPE ORGAN, CELLOS, SHAKUHACHI, BOW + PLUCK, BASS / PIANO,
  DEEP CHOIR, HIT ARP, BRASS / HIT, AIR CHOIR, CELLO>CHOIR, HARP + BELL.
- **A power-on with a record**: it turns, the tone arm sets down, DITTO comes up.
- **USB rescue**: OCT− held at power-on, or after two failed starts.
